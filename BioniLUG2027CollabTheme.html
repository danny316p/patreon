For 2027, we have an exceptionally broad theme that we look forward to spamming across wide areas in our quest for world domination. A punny name hides a variety of inspirations and great tastes that taste great together. The BioniLUG 2027 collaboration theme is...

Dungeness and Dragons

First inspiration: In recognition of the LEGO House's 10th anniversary, a few of us were interested in building our own dragon airports (dragonports, if you will) similar in concept to the one featured on the LEGO House's Tree of Creativity. Planes and airports come in a wide variety of shapes and sizes, and it's rare that runways are close together - much like members of a LUG that organizes online. All scales and styles are welcome. Build any dragon or dragon-based scene preparing for takeoff - maybe they're preparing to fly off to LEGO House, maybe they're visiting some other magical land.

...or maybe they're crabby. Airport security is unpleasant at any scale.

Which brings us to our second inspiration: Stabby Crabs! Following some colorful commentary about BrickFair's Baltimore event, it was observed in another Discord server that "no one got 🔪 by a 🦀". BioniLUG's acting Secretary of Events & Exhibitions joked that that could be next year's collaboration theme - and with the recurrent popularity of crabs as a constraction build theme, the oldie but goodie had no problem getting votes for our real theme. While we started with "stabby", we're not requiring (or expecting) the crabs to be violent. Crabs of any size, style, and attitude are welcome.

That led to discussions about doing two themes, or combining themes... crabs and dragons. Drabs or Cragons? Crab Dragons. "Dragonports, Stabby Crabs, or any combination thereof". Crabs vs Dragons.

Ultimately, we decided: Yes. All of them. All of the crabs, all of the dragons, all of the goofy surrounding scenes and combinations of the things. Then came the pun: Dungeness & Dragons. We picked it to be snappy and memorable, but we're encouraging nearly everything dragon- or crab-based, regardless of how close it is to the initial inspirations. We're sure you can come up with something fun to build incorporating any, some, or all of these ideas. We will only be disappointed if no one builds a crab riding a dragon.
