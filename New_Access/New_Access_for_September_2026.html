New Access for September 2026

These posts have become wildly out-of-sync as life gets too busy to focus on Patreon. There are plenty of things queued up that I should work on granting access to, but my backlog doesn't feel like it's getting any shorter - and a mix of surgery scheduling and general uncertainly in life continues to pull me away from the things I care about. For this post, I will be focusing on newly available material from last year's Le Sommet de la Brique, a small but substantial francophone AFOL convention in Saint-Hyacinthe, Quebec, Canada. I will miss this year's event, but you can still buy public tickets or register for the full convention: it runs October 1st-4th this year.
