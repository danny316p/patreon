Upcoming Events (Fall 2026)

It's been a hectic few months here, with decidedly less glamorous goings-on between returning from Billund in June and getting to Brickomanie a few weeks back. I probably should have plugged Brickomanie here! Besides being a great opportunity to represent BioniLUG and hang out with members of QueLUG, ToroLUG, and more, it also saw the debut of my "Places to See in Billund" slides that I prepared for this year's BrickCon.

...which brings me to my first upcoming event to share:
BrickCon 2026: AFOL convention in greater Seattle and online https://afol.brickcon.org/ (September 10th-13th)

I will be attending virtually and presenting on Sunday, September 13th, at 6 PM EDT (3 PM convention time). While I'd like to think that anyone following me here likely follows my Flickr photostream and/or Discord server already, there's a good chance that you've never seen some of the photos and rarities I'm planning to present. I'm also looking into making use of the virtual format to show off some goodies that I don't travel with.

My late September-October is intentionally blank at the moment to focus on packing work and expected (but not yet scheduled) surgery. So far, my carpal tunnel syndrome and cubital tunnel syndrome have only been addressed on my left side, but my nerves were completely messed up on both sides. I should know more after my next follow-up appointment on September 14th.

Early November is extremely busy. Unfortunately, I will have to miss the recently relocated Hunt Valley Holiday Teddy Bear Show & Sale because of two other events overlapping that weekend.

BrickFair PA 2026: AFOL convention in Oaks, PA https://www.brickfair.com/ (Thursday, November 5th; Friday; portions of Saturday; and Sunday, November 8th)
BLYTHECON USA/Wonderland of Play Philadelphia Seaport Edition: "you can count on fun, friendship, lots of BLYTHE dolls... and toys and teds too!" https://www.wonderlandofplay.com/playdates-london-ny (Saturday November 7th 11 AM - 2 PM Mascot-making workshop hosted by Jody Battaglia ONLY) - I was excited to see that Jody Battaglia is going to be there. I started collecting her Boyds' Bears designs almost 25 years ago, and more recently I spotted some of her FunTown dolls in Billund's Teddy Bear Art Museum (note that this photo is from 2019 - they were not on display while I was there in 2026). I'm a little nervous about my own sewing skills, but should be able to enjoy the 3 hour class anyway.
...at least both of those are relatively near each other in the greater Philadelphia area, so I can attend part of both.
